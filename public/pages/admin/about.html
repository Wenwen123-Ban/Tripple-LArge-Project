About Click & Collect

Click & Collect is the Library Borrowing and Assistance System (LBAS) developed for North Western Mindanao State College of Science and Technology (NMSC-ST).

The system allows students to reserve and borrow books online, and provides librarians with tools to manage the library catalog, user accounts, circulation transactions, and operational rules.
