Help & FAQ

Getting Started

How do I access the library catalog?

Simply navigate to the Home section using the Home icon in the sidebar. You'll see a grid of available books with their status indicators.

How do I search for a specific book?

Use the search bar at the top of the Home page to search by title, author, or keywords. You can also filter by category using the category dropdown.

Reservations & Borrowing

How do I reserve a book?

Click the "Reserve" button on a book card that is available. You'll receive a notification when the book is ready for pickup.

What does each status color mean?

Green: Book is available for reservation
Orange: Book is currently reserved by another user
Red: Book is currently borrowed by another user

How long can I borrow a book?

Standard borrowing period is 14 days from the date of pickup. Check the Manage section for your specific due dates.

My Account

How do I view my digital library card?

Click the Card icon in the sidebar to access your Digital Library Card which contains your account information and borrowing history.

How do I manage my loans and reservations?

Use the Manage section (book icon in the sidebar) to view your current reservations, borrowed books, and borrowing history.

How do I update my account information?

Visit the Digital Library Card section to view and manage your profile information.

Notifications

How do I view my notifications?

Click the Notifications icon in the sidebar to access your notifications. Here you'll see updates about reservations and due dates.

How can I clear old notifications?

In the Notifications section, use the "Clear" button to remove old messages.

Troubleshooting

I can't find a specific book. What should I do?

Try using different keywords in the search bar or browsing different categories. Contact library staff if you need assistance locating a specific book.

My reservation is not showing up. What should I do?

Wait a few moments for the system to process your request. If it still doesn't appear, try refreshing the page or contacting library support.

I forgot when my book is due. How can I check?

Visit the Manage section to see all your borrowed books with their due dates.

Still Need Help?

If you cannot find the answer to your question, please contact the library staff at the North Western Mindanao State College of Science and Technology library desk or visit in person during library hours.
