About Click & Collect

Click & Collect is a modern digital library management system designed to make book borrowing and management convenient and efficient. This platform allows students and faculty to browse, search, reserve, and manage their book borrowing needs through an intuitive web interface.

Key Features

  • Browse and search our comprehensive library catalog
  • View real-time book availability status
  • Reserve books online before visiting the library
  • Manage your current loans and reservations
  • Generate digital library card
  • Receive notifications about your reservations and due dates
  • Track your borrowing history

Institution

North Western Mindanao State College of Science and Technology (NMSCST) operates this digital library system to provide accessible resources to our academic community.

Contact & Support

For questions or assistance with the Click & Collect system, please visit the Help section or contact the library staff directly.
